01 · The brand

What it is

The Long Play turns a child’s first years of guitar into a lifelong relationship with music. Not lessons. A relationship.

The name carries

Music, natively — LP, a record, play. And the long game: a year-long program, and a relationship meant to outlast it. “Studio” was the room. The Long Play is the life.

Who it’s for

A year-long, founder-led music program for children 7–14 in Pune, taught by one person: Akshit Deshmukh. Small cohort, made by hand.

It is

A relationship with music. Play first. One real recording by June. A teacher who knows your child.

It is not

A pay-per-lesson music class. An exam-grade factory. A premium-gloss “experience.”

The mark 3 versions

A record groove that doubles as a tree-ring — the long game — over Akshit’s own hand. Use the version that sits best on the background. Never recolour outside these three.

Black · one-colour, print & stamps
Groove mark — favicon, avatar, stamp
Clear space: keep the height of the groove mark clear on all sides.
Minimum size: 120px wide on screen; 24mm in print. Below that, use the groove mark alone.
Don’t stretch, rotate, add shadows, or outline it.
Don’t recolour it or place the black version on a busy/dark background.

Field & Studio

Forest green grounds the brand, cream gives it air, and gold is a rare highlight — never a second colour. Warm and crafted, never glossy.

Green for grounds, headers, the logo field.
Cream / paper #F4F0E4 for backgrounds and breathing space.
Gold is an accent — max ~10%. Never a second primary; overuse tips “premium gloss.”
Ink #3A3A36 for body text — passes WCAG AA on cream.
Moss for support / positive states — use sparingly.
Minimum body text 15px on screen.

Two typefaces

English only. The logo is the custom hand — these fonts do everything else. Both free (OFL): zero licensing friction.

Fraunces — display
Play first. Read later.

Headlines, taglines, big moments. Warm, characterful, slightly old-style — use the softer optical cut, not the sharp high-contrast one.

Inter — body
By June, your child will have made one real recording. You’ll have it to keep. Clean, neutral, parent-legible — the workhorse for body, captions, and interface.

Body copy, web, forms, captions.

Fraunces for display + tagline only.
Inter for all running text.
Generous line-height (1.5–1.6) for warmth.
Sentence case — avoid ALL-CAPS shouting.

05 · Voice

How it sounds

A working musician who knows your child — warm, plain, composed. Tone 3 · Language 2 · Personality 3 · Emotion 2.5.

1 · Specific over abstract

Do — “Deana, 11, closed the AI app and said: there’s more creativity in doing it myself.”
Don’t — “We help young learners rediscover their passion for music.”

2 · Show the work, don’t sell it

Do — “By June, your child will have made one real recording. You’ll have it to keep.”
Don’t — “An amazing, premium, transformative experience your child deserves.”

3 · Play first, then the technical

Do — “First they play. When it serves the music, they learn to read it.”
Don’t — “Our structured curriculum builds music theory from grade one.”

Lead line + alternates

Play first. Read later. A relationship with music that outlasts the lessons. Music, not just guitar.
