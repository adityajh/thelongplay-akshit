What it costs, and how it works.

A relationship with music that outlasts the lessons.

The Long Play is sold as a year, not by the hour. One fee covers everything in the program — the weekly teaching, the recording, the performances, the portfolio. There are two ways in, depending on how lessons reach your child. Founding families who join this first cohort pay a reduced rate for the first six months.
